# Banners & Ribbons Specification

Used for marketing announcements or critical account alerts (e.g., "Overdue Fees").

## 1. Top Banners (Dismissible)
*   Full width across the top of the screen (above or below the Nav Bar).
*   **Background:** Solid vibrant color (Gold `#D4AF37` for marketing, Red `#EF4444` for critical alerts) to intentionally break the dark theme and draw immediate attention.
*   **Text:** Black (`#000000`) or White (`#FFFFFF`) depending on contrast requirements. Center-aligned.
*   **Close Icon:** Small 'x' on the far right.

## 2. Corner Ribbons
*   Used on Cards (e.g., a "New!" ribbon on a feature card).
*   Positioned top-right or top-left, rotated 45 degrees.
*   **Background:** Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`).
