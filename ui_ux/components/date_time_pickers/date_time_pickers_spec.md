# Date & Time Pickers Specification

Crucial for the timetable, exam scheduling, and attendance modules.

## 1. Input Field
*   Follows the standard **Forms Specification** (Background `#121212`, Text `#FFFFFF`).
*   **Icon:** A calendar or clock icon on the right side (`#A1A1AA`).

## 2. Popup Calendar (Date Picker)
*   **Background:** Deep Dark Grey (`#1E1E1E`), Border Radius `8px`, Drop Shadow.
*   **Header:** Month/Year text (`#FFFFFF`) with left/right arrows (`#A1A1AA`).
*   **Days of the Week:** Small text (`0.75rem`), `#A1A1AA`.
*   **Day Cells:**
    *   Default: `#FFFFFF` on hover `#2A2A2A`.
    *   Selected: Background `#D4AF37`, Text `#000000`.
    *   Today: Text `#D4AF37`, subtle border.
    *   Out of Month: Opacity `0.3`.
