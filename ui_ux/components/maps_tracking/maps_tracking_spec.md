# Maps & GPS Tracking Specification

Highly specific to the Transport Management module for live bus tracking.

## 1. Map Canvas Styling
*   **Map Theme:** Dark Mode Map (e.g., Google Maps Dark or Mapbox Dark styling) to seamlessly blend with the `#000000` Nexora aesthetic.
*   **Roads:** Dark greys.
*   **Water:** Very dark blue/charcoal.

## 2. Overlays & Markers
*   **Vehicle Marker:** A custom icon (bus/van) filled with Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`).
*   **Route Line:** A solid line drawn on the map. Color: Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`) or a vibrant Cyan (`#45B7D1`) for contrast.
*   **Stops/Waypoints:** White circular dots with a dark outline.

## 3. Info Window (Popup on Marker Click)
*   Follows the **Tooltips & Popovers Specification**.
*   Displays Speed, ETA, and Current Driver in small, high-contrast text.
