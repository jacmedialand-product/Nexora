# Lists & List Items Specification

Used in settings menus, notification panels, and mobile contact directories.

## 1. List Container
*   **Background:** `#1E1E1E` (Card surface).
*   **Border Radius:** `8px` or `16px` depending on context.

## 2. List Item
*   **Padding:** `12px 16px`.
*   **Hover State:** Background lightens to `#2A2A2A`.
*   **Separator:** `1px solid #333333` between items, but not after the last item.
*   **Content Layout:** Usually an Icon/Avatar on the left, primary text in the center (White `#FFFFFF`), and an action or chevron on the far right (`#A1A1AA`).
